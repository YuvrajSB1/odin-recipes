Golgappa

About Golgappa

Golgappa (also widely known as Panipuri or Phuchka) is a highly popular, bite-sized street food snack native to the Indian subcontinent, famous for delivering a literal burst of sweet, spicy, and tangy flavours in a single mouthful.

Ingredients

  • Semolina (Suji) or whole wheat puris
  • Boiled and mashed potatoes
  • Boiled black chickpeas
  • Fresh mint and coriander leaves
  • Tamarind paste and jaggery
  • Chaat masala and roasted cumin powder

Steps

  1. Gently poke a small hole in the center of each crisp, hollow puri.
  2. Stuff a small amount of the mashed potato and chickpea mixture inside.
  3. Prepare the spiced water by blending mint, coriander, and green chillies with water.
  4. Add tamarind pulp and jaggery to a separate bowl of water for the sweet option.
  5. Dip the stuffed puri into the chilled, flavored water.
  6. Serve immediately and eat whole in one single bite!
