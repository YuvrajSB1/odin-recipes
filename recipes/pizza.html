Pizza

About pizza

Pizza is a savory dish made of a flattened wheat bread dough base topped with tomato sauce, cheese (usually mozzarella), and various meats, vegetables, or spices, then baked at a high temperature

Ingredients

  • Dough: 2 cups all-purpose flour, 1 packet (2 1/4 teaspoons) active dry yeast, 3/4 cup warm water, 1 teaspoon sugar, 1 teaspoon salt, and 1 tablespoon olive oil.
  • Sauce & Toppings: 1/2 cup tomato sauce or pizza sauce, 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese, 1 tablespoon olive oil, and optional toppings like sliced onions, bell peppers, or mushrooms.

Steps

  1. Activate the yeast by mixing the warm water, sugar, and yeast in a small bowl. Let it rest for 5 minutes until the top becomes foamy.
  2. Make the dough by mixing the flour and salt in a large bowl, then pouring in the yeast mixture and olive oil.
  3. Knead the dough on a clean, floured surface for about 8 to 10 minutes until it feels smooth and stretchy.
  4. Let the dough rise in a lightly oiled bowl covered with a clean cloth for 1 to 2 hours, or until it doubles in size.
  5. Preheat your oven to a very hot temperature, around 450°F (230°C). If you have a baking stone, place it inside to heat up.
  6. Shape the dough by pressing it out with your hands or rolling it with a pin on a floured surface into a flat, round circle.
  7. Add the toppings by spreading a thin layer of tomato sauce over the dough, scattering the mozzarella cheese evenly, and adding your favorite vegetables or meats.
  8. Bake the pizza for 12 to 15 minutes until the crust turns golden brown and the cheese bubbles.
